## 🎓 Key Features Covered

- **Driving License Management**: Online applications, exam scheduling, digital licenses with QR codes
- **Vehicle Registration**: Seamless registration and ownership transfer workflows  
- **Route Permits**: Multi-step approval system for transport operators
- **Payment Gateway**: Secure online payments with SSLCommerz integration
- **AI Chatbot**: 24/7 multilingual assistant powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite
- **Police Portal**: Real-time verification and roadside enforcement tools
- **Admin Dashboard**: Comprehensive management and analytics platform
